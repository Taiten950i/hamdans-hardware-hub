About Me

Welcome to Hamdan’s Hardware Hub! My name is Hamdan Almehairbi (known online as Taiten / Taiten95). I’m a tech enthusiast based in the United Arab Emirates with a huge passion for video game history, hardware collecting, and system software customization.

My primary focus is on **building a physical collection of gaming consoles and tech devices** and exploring their true capabilities. I specialize in **softmodding and jailbreaking**—using custom firmware (CFW), homebrew environments, and system software tweaks to bypass factory restrictions and unlock the full potential of every machine in my collection.
